GlitchTip is ready at $CLOUDRON-APP-ORIGIN.

<sso>
Sign in with Cloudron. That first account is the administrator. Create an organization in the app. Later Cloudron accounts can sign in too.
</sso>

<nosso>
Open $CLOUDRON-APP-ORIGIN and create the first account. That person creates the organization. Public signup closes after that account.
</nosso>

Uploads are stored in /app/data/media.

Optional settings are in /app/data/env.sh. Restart the app after editing that file. Set ENABLE_SOCIAL_APPS_USER_REGISTRATION=false there to close Cloudron sign-up after the first account.
